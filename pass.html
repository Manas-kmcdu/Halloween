The Brief · Halloween House

THE BIGGEST COLLEGE HALLOWEEN PARTY.

Every year, on this one night, the line between the dead and the living goes thin. The dead come looking for a body to borrow. The living, who have been dodging things since their first attendance sheet, found the oldest trick there is: put on a costume and nothing will possess you.

This year the dead have gone to college. They are in the back row, the library stacks, the canteen queue and the group chat that nobody replies to. Some never graduated. Some never left.

The one safe place on campus tonight is the Halloween House. Walk in, walk through the rooms, and take whatever you came for. Just be someone else when you do.

COME DRESSED. STAY DISGUISED. DON'T GET POSSESSED.
